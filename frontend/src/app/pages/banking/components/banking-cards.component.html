Cards and Digital Wallets

Once your Swedish bank account is active, you'll usually receive a debit card. You may also be eligible for a credit card and can connect your cards to Apple Pay, Google Wallet or Samsung Wallet.

Debit Card

Money is taken directly from your bank account. This is the most common card used in Sweden for everyday purchases.

  • Salary account
  • ATM withdrawals
  • Online shopping
  • Apple Pay & Google Wallet

Credit Card

Borrow money from the bank and repay later. Often includes travel insurance, cashback and purchase protection.

  • Interest after due date
  • Credit limit
  • Travel benefits
  • Purchase protection

Popular Cards in Sweden

Klarna Card

Buy Now Pay Later

Popular for online shopping with flexible payment options and purchase tracking.

Bank Debit Cards

Everyday banking

Issued by your Swedish bank and accepted almost everywhere across Sweden.

Mastercard & Visa

Worldwide acceptance

Nearly every Swedish bank issues Visa or Mastercard debit and credit cards.

Digital Wallets

Most Swedish banks allow you to add your debit or credit card to your mobile wallet.

Apple Pay

Tap your iPhone or Apple Watch to pay securely.

Google Wallet

Fast and secure payments on Android devices.

Samsung Wallet

Available on supported Samsung devices.

Good to know

Contactless payments are accepted almost everywhere in Sweden. Many shops no longer accept cash, so carrying a debit card or mobile wallet is highly recommended.

  • ✔ Always enable contactless payments.
  • ✔ Keep your PIN secure.
  • ✔ Freeze your card immediately if lost.
  • ✔ Never share your BankID verification codes.
