Before you begin

Cashless Society

Sweden is one of the world's most cashless countries. Cards and Swish are accepted almost everywhere.

Why You Need A Bank

You'll need a Swedish bank account for salary, taxes, BankID and many public services.

Before Applying

Prepare your passport, residence permit, Personnummer and Swedish ID.
