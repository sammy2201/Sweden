Open your first Swedish bank account

Opening a Swedish bank account is one of the most important steps after receiving your Personnummer. Follow the process below.

1

Choose a Bank

Compare different Swedish banks and choose one that matches your needs.

2

Prepare Documents

Make sure you have the required documents before booking your visit.

3

Visit the Bank

Most banks require an appointment. Bring original documents.

4

Identity Verification

The bank verifies your identity and performs regulatory checks.

5

Receive Account

You'll receive your account details, debit card and internet banking access.

Required Documents

  • Passport
  • Residence Permit
  • Personnummer
  • Swedish ID Card (if available)
  • Employment Contract (sometimes requested)
  • University Admission Letter (students)

Good to Know

  • Some banks require appointments.
  • Bring original documents.
  • Processing may take several days.
  • Banks may request additional information.
