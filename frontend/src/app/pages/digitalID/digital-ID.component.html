Digital Identity

Sweden relies on digital identification for almost every online service. Learn how BankID and Freja eID+ work, when you need them, and how to get started.

Overview BankID Freja eID+ Comparison

What is Digital Identity?

Digital identity lets you securely prove who you are online. In Sweden it's required for banking, healthcare, taxes and many government services.

Why is it important?

Without digital identification you'll find it difficult to access many essential online services after moving to Sweden.

Which one should I choose?

Most residents use BankID. Freja eID+ is an excellent alternative if BankID isn't available.

BankID

BankID is Sweden's most widely used digital identification solution. It is issued through participating Swedish banks.

How to get BankID

BankID is issued by participating Swedish banks. Before you can activate BankID, you'll usually need a Swedish personal identity number and a bank account.

1

When should I apply?

Apply after receiving your Personnummer and opening a Swedish bank account.

2

Where do I apply?

Visit your bank or use the bank's mobile application if you're already a customer.

3

How do I activate it?

Your bank will verify your identity and guide you through the activation process.

You'll usually need

  • Personnummer
  • Swedish bank account
  • Valid passport or Swedish ID
  • Registered mobile device

How to get Freja eID+

Freja eID+ is an approved Swedish digital identity that doesn't require a Swedish bank account.

1

Download the app

Install Freja from the App Store or Google Play.

2

Create your account

Register your information inside the application.

3

Verify your identity

Complete identity verification to upgrade to Freja eID+.

You'll usually need

  • Passport or National ID
  • Smartphone
  • Email address
  • Identity verification
