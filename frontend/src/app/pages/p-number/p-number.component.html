Personal Number

The Swedish Personal Identity Number (Personnummer) is your key to living in Sweden. It is required for healthcare, banking, employment, taxes, education and many public services.

Quick overview

What is it?

Your permanent identification number used across Sweden by public authorities, banks and employers.

Who needs one?

Anyone planning to live in Sweden for at least one year usually needs a Personnummer.

Where do I apply?

Apply in person at your nearest Skatteverket office after arriving in Sweden.
